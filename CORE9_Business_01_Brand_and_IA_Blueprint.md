# **1\. ENTITY & BRAND HIERARCHY**

## **1.1 The Core Identity**

* **Brand Name:** CORE9 RESOLVE™ Business  
* **Purpose:** Convert a business leader's recognition of an organizational problem into an initial Discovery conversation.  
* **Brand Positioning:** CORE9 RESOLVE™ is a proprietary whole-system methodology. Its Business application identifies hidden structural and functional disconnects, reveals opportunities, and provides strategic guidance for coordinated execution.  
* **Central Premise:** Every organization is a dynamic living organism where parts communicate with one another. Rather than offering isolated fixes for single symptoms, CORE9 RESOLVE™ examines the relationships between leadership, people, processes, resources, and execution.

## **1.2 Separation of Worlds**

* **Door 1 (CORE9 RESOLVE™ Business):** Structured, executive, analytical, and architectural. Built for business leaders, founders, and decision-makers.  
* **Door 2 (CORE9 RESOLVE™ / SAGE):** Separate application and experiential digital playground for human potential. SAGE is NOT buried inside the Business landing page; it has its own distinct entrance and experience.

# **4\. THE 4-LEVEL PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE ARCHITECTURE**

The landing page is the front door, not the entire building. Visitors go deeper only when they choose to; a leader can book a Discovery session directly from Level 1\.

* **LEVEL 1 — PUBLIC ENTRY (Stage 1 Build):**  
  * CORE9 RESOLVE™ Business Landing Page (Problem \-\> Promise \-\> Recognition \-\> Difference \-\> Pathway \-\> Action).  
* **LEVEL 2 — CORE CONTEXT (Stage 2 Build):**  
  * Our Approach: The methodology, diagnostic perspective, and how we work.  
  * Our Team: Authorship, expertise, roles, and collaboration (also functions as a standalone digital business/visiting card link).  
* **LEVEL 3 — DEEPER UNDERSTANDING (Stage 3 Build):**  
  * The CORE9 System: Nine Intelligences, Four Spheres, Living-System Architecture, Diagnostics, Recognition, and Functional Alignment (CORE • NINE • RESOLVE).  
* **LEVEL 4 — ENGAGEMENT & RESOURCES (Stage 4 Build):**  
  * How We Work: Discovery \-\> Intensive \-\> Strategic Guidance, Performance Map, 30/60/90-Day Roadmap, Executive Reset, Ongoing Advisory.  
  * Insights & Resources: Video/Audio Broadcast Blog (5–7 minute explainers), articles, book chapters, and explanatory documents for SEO presence.
